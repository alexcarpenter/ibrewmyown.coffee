## Who are you and what do you do?

My name is Stacey Carpenter, I am happily married to Alex and am a stay at home mom. Before we had our son, Micah, I worked for a car dealership and a barista before that. Although it’s been over 10 years since working in the coffee industry, I still enjoy geeking out about it.

## What does your brewing setup look like?

Currently I use a Moccamaster for that first cup in the morning. It may sound a bit contradictory, but I love to take the mornings slow and I enjoy having a great tasting cup of coffee that I can be drinking in less time it takes to boil water on the stove.

In the afternoon I typically make a cortado on our Rocket Apartmento TCA machine we got this past winter. I like making a drink that has a bit of steamed milk in it to see if I can still pour decent latte art.

## What is your prefered brewing method?

The Kalita Wave has been a favorite of mine for a long time. The rhythmic pouring motion you get with a pour over feels a bit meditative. I also enjoy using a Chemex because it was one of the first pour overs I learned to make when I worked at a coffee shop back in 2011.

## Lastly, any coffee bean recommendations?

We’ve been using Trade Coffee’s Subscription since late 2022 and haven’t looked back since. Being able to try new beans from different roasters around the US has been really fun. You get to plug in your taste preferences and get recommendations based on that.

Of course if we are out and about we like to grab a bag from a local roaster.
